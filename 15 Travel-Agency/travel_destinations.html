Tourist Destinations

MANALI

A beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh, known for snow-capped mountains, the Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass and cool weather all year.

GOA

India's famous beach destination with golden sands, water sports, old churches, lively markets and exciting nightlife.

JAIPUR

The Pink City of Rajasthan, famous for Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace and colourful bazaars full of handicrafts.
